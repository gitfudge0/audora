# Audora Logo Usage

## Core idea
Audora uses a monochrome-first album-cover mark with a hidden negative-space “A” and a subtle disc cue. The mark is designed to feel premium, warm, personal, and slightly nostalgic without looking retro or decorative.

## Logo versions
- Use `audora-logo-horizontal-*` for app splash screens, website headers, Play Store feature graphics, and in-app branding.
- Use `audora-mark-*` for compact brand moments, empty states, loading screens, and monochrome usage.
- Use `audora-app-icon-*` for launcher icons, social avatars, favicons, and store icons.

## Clear space
Keep at least one disc radius of clear space around the standalone mark. For the horizontal lockup, keep at least the width of the icon’s central disc between the logo and any surrounding element.

## Minimum sizes
- Standalone mark: 24 px minimum.
- Horizontal logo: 120 px minimum width.
- Favicon: use the provided 16/32/48 px exports.

## Typography
The wordmark uses a clean geometric sans direction. The SVG lockups reference `Inter Display, Inter, Manrope, Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`. Do not redistribute font files with the brand assets.

## Do not
- Do not add gradients to the core logo.
- Do not curve or bend the wordmark.
- Do not place the black mark on a low-contrast dark background.
- Do not redraw the A with inward-curving feet; the A should meet the lower edge of the mark.
- Do not add extra music notes, waveforms, or decorative sparkles.
