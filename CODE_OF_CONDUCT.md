# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Audora should be a practical, respectful project for people improving local music-library tooling. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and clarity.
- Keep disagreement focused on the technical decision, user impact, and project goals.
- Respect different experience levels, devices, operating systems, languages, and accessibility needs.
- Avoid harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualized language, discriminatory remarks, and sustained off-topic disruption.

## Project-Specific Norms

Audora writes to local music files, so maintainers may be strict about evidence, reproduction steps, and safety. That scrutiny is about protecting users' libraries, not about gatekeeping.

Good participation includes:

- Sharing logs, screenshots, format details, and exact reproduction steps when reporting bugs.
- Avoiding requests to exchange copyrighted music files.
- Being honest about uncertainty, risk, and incomplete testing.
- Accepting maintainers' decisions about product scope and file-safety requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

Enforcement aims to protect contributors and keep the project usable. Actions may include:

- A clarification or warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary loss of issue or pull request participation.
- Permanent removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or notice unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers. Include links, screenshots, usernames, dates, and any relevant context.

Reports will be handled with care and shared only with people needed to review and respond.
